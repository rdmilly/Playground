FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

What's included in bi-weekly maintenance?
Each visit includes professional mowing with clean lines, edging along sidewalks and driveways, string trimming around obstacles, and thorough blowing of all hard surfaces. We also spot-treat weeds and monitor your lawn's health.
How large of properties do you service?
We specialize in residential properties and small acreage up to 5 acres. This includes standard suburban lots, larger rural properties, and everything in between. For acreage clearing, we can handle lots up to 5 acres.
Can I cancel my maintenance contract?
Yes! We offer hassle-free cancellation. You can cancel with 30 days notice. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we'll refund your payment in full - no questions asked. We're confident you'll love our service.
What if it rains on my scheduled day?
We automatically reschedule for the next available day at no extra charge. Contract members get priority rescheduling to ensure your lawn stays on track. We'll always notify you of any schedule changes via text or email.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely! We carry $1 million in general liability insurance and are fully licensed in all service areas. All our team members are background-checked and trained professionals. Your property is always protected.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, ACH bank transfers, and checks. Contract members can set up convenient auto-pay for hassle-free billing. One-time services can be paid upon completion.
